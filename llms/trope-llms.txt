# Trope

> Trope is the AI-native platform that helps ERP implementation partners deliver successful implementations faster.

Trope is built for ERP implementation partners — including Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central consultancies — and handles the delivery work that erodes margins: discovery, requirements, fit-gap analysis, data migration, approval workflows, UAT scripts, training guides, documentation, and go-live readiness, in one connected system.
